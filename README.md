# Hands-on Project: Generative AI for Data Visualization

How to use Generative AI to prepare a report for your data using charts and insights, in short Storytelling or Visualization
Source: [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/generative-ai-elevate-your-data-science-career/ungradedWidget/sB26L/hands-on-lab-generative-ai-for-data-visualization)

### Objectives:

- Sign up for Zoho Analytics and upload a dataset.
- Generate a report using Zia AI prompts.


### Lab Overview

In this lab, we will learn how to use Generative AI to prepare a data report using charts and insights.

To begin, create an account on the [Zoho Analytics website](https://www.zoho.com/analytics/).

### Task 1: Sign Up for Zoho Analytics

**Step 1:** Visit the [Zoho Analytics website](https://www.zoho.com/analytics/).

**Step 2:** Fill in the required information and click the **SIGN UP FOR FREE** button.

> **Note:** You can sign up for Zoho Analytics using various options, such as Google, LinkedIn, or a Zoho email account. The platform offers a 15-day free trial without requiring credit card details.

### Task 2: Upload a Dataset

**Step 1:** Download the dataset by holding down the **Ctrl** key on your keyboard and clicking the [here](https://cf-courses-data.s3.us.cloud-object-storage.appdomain.cloud/IBMSkillsNetwork-AI0272EN-SkillsNetwork/labs/dataset/electronics_dataset.csv) link.

**Step 2:** On your Zoho Analytics homepage, select **Local Drive**.

### **Step 3:** Enter the **Workspace Name** and click **Choose file**.

**Step 4:** Select and upload the `electronics_dataset.csv` file. Now, click **Next**.

### Task 3: Using Zia AI to explore data and generate reports

**Step 1:** Go to the left corner, click **More** and select **Ask Zia** option.

**Step 2:** Enter the following prompts one by one in the Zia AI prompt bar and generate all the charts through Zia.

**Prompt 1:**

`Show a line chart of total revenue grouped by month using the Order Date column.`

You can click **Zia insights** icon on the top-right corner to see the detailed insights of the generated chart as shown below.

**Prompt 2:**

`Create a bar chart of the top 10 products by total revenue.`

**Prompt 3:**

`Create a pie chart showing each product's contribution to total revenue.`

**Prompt 4:**

`Create a pivot table with product name, total quantity ordered, and total revenue.`

**Step 3:** After generating all charts go to **Dashboard** and select **Create new Dashboard**

**Step 4:** Drag and drop the dataset in the report section.

### Task 4: Finalizing the charts to be used in the report

**Step 1:** Go back to Zia, save, and add the charts to the dashboard one by one that you created earlier.

Repeat the same step to add the other charts to the dashboard.

**Step 2:** Once the charts are added, go to the dashboard to find the charts that you have added.

### Task 5: Saving and sharing the report

**Step 1:** Go to the **View Mode**

**Step 2:** To export the dashboard, click **Export** and select the appropriate option to download the dashboard.

**Step 3:** You can also share it with your colleagues and peers. To do so, click **Share** and then select the option as per your convenience.

## Conclusion

You have signed up in Zoho Analytics, added a dataset (electronic_dataset.csv), and generated a report.
